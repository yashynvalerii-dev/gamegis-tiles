GameGIS Tiles

Static, pre-processed OpenStreetMap geodata for client applications: buildings (with courtyards), walkways, entrances, water, railways, power lines and restricted areas, grouped into small bundles.

  • Index: manifest.json
  • Client: gamegis-client.js (mirrors, checksum, gzip, tile status)
  • Bundles: bundles/13/{x}/{y}.json.gz (gzip; Web Mercator z13; each holds 16 z15 tiles with a READY/INCOMPLETE status)
